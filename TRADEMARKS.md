## Using the name and logos

Truthful references to Freehand for attribution, provenance, compatibility,
documentation, reviews, and discussion are welcome.

Ordinary development forks may retain the repository name. Mirrors of unmodified
official releases and accurate package-manager listings may identify the software
as Freehand. These uses should accurately identify the distributor and any
changes, without suggesting that an independently operated repository, mirror,
or package is maintained or endorsed by the Freehand project.

This policy does not grant permission to use the Freehand name or logos in ways
likely to mislead users about a product's origin, sponsorship, endorsement, or
affiliation with the original project.

For independently maintained products, we ask distributors to use a distinct
product name and visual identity, identify their maintainer, and explain their
relationship to Freehand. A truthful description such as "Based on Freehand" is
welcome. This request is project guidance, not an additional condition of the MIT
License.
