Choose a task from the activity rail on the left. Each task has its own
connection and model settings; only Voice needs a microphone. If you have not
configured a service yet, start with [Get started](../../getting-started/).

| I want to… | Open | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dictate into another application | **Voice transcription** | Text inserted while the captured destination is still safe, or available to copy |
| Transcribe an existing recording | **Audio file** | A transcript you copy explicitly |
| Hear text read aloud | **Text to speech** | Audio you can play and explicitly save |

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> on Windows or <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> on
macOS to find a task or settings page. The rail also opens **Connections**,
**Local runtime**, **History**, and **Settings**.

## Voice dictation

Complete the **Voice** readiness screen with a usable microphone and transcription
service. A recording shortcut is optional, but lets you begin with another app's
text field focused. See [first dictation setup](../../getting-started/#run-your-first-dictation).

<Steps>

1. **Focus the destination text field.** Keep this application and window open.
2. **Start recording** with your toggle or hold-to-talk shortcut. Wait for
   Freehand to show that recording has started, then speak.
3. **Stop recording** with the toggle shortcut, or release the held chord.
4. **Keep the destination focused** while transcription and any enabled cleanup
   finish. The completed text appears there when delivery is safe.

</Steps>

:::caution[Check before pasting a recovery result]
If delivery stops, use **Copy** on the current result. Some text may already have
been inserted: check the destination before pasting to avoid duplicates. Windows
requires the original focused control; macOS allows another field in the same
app and window. See [safe insertion](../privacy-and-safety/#safe-text-insertion).
:::

To change inputs, choose **Microphone** at the top of the Voice sidebar. The
selection saves immediately for the next recording. **System default microphone**
follows the system setting; **Refresh microphones** finds a newly connected
device. A disconnected saved device stays selected until you reconnect or replace it.

### Toggle recording

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Space</kbd> to start and press it
again to stop, unless you have changed the shortcut. With voice detection and
automatic stop enabled, a pause after speech can also finish the recording.

You can **Clear** the toggle in **Settings → Shortcuts** and use on-screen
controls instead. Starting in Freehand may require copying the result because
the intended destination was not focused at recording start.

### Hold to talk

Assign a chord in **Settings → Shortcuts**, hold it while speaking, then release
it to finish. Automatic silence ending does not stop a held recording. Supported
modifier-only combinations and conflict recovery are covered in
[Keyboard shortcuts](../../reference/shortcuts/).

### Long dictation and VAD

Voice activity detection (VAD) identifies speech and silence on your computer and
is on by default. These optional controls in **Voice transcription → Audio**
start off:

| Control | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Trim silence** | Removes silence from the beginning and end |
| **Stop after I finish speaking** | Ends a toggle recording after the configured pause |
| **Split long dictation on silence** | Transcribes checkpoints while you continue; prefers pauses and can split at the duration limit |

Checkpoint transcripts are assembled and delivered together when you finish.
For a preview that changes while you speak, use [Live transcription](../live-transcription/).

## Audio files

Open **Audio file**, choose a recording, then choose **Transcribe**. File selection
does not upload anything until you start. Results always need explicit **Copy**;
you do not need a microphone or completed Voice setup.

## Text to speech

Open **Text to speech** to generate audio from your own text. After enabling a
speech connection, model, and voice, choose **Speak**. You can also **Listen** to
a completed transcript, with history on or off.

<LinkCard title="Generate and play speech" href="../text-to-speech/" description="Set up a voice, preview it, control playback, and save generated audio." />

## Transcript cleanup

Optional **Cleanup**, shared by Voice and audio files, sends the raw transcript
to a separate chat service. Keep it off for unchanged text, use custom
instructions, or choose S1-mini's English cleanup controls. A failure, timeout,
or empty response falls back to raw text.

<LinkCard title="Set up transcript cleanup" href="../post-processing/" description="Choose a processor, understand language restrictions, and compare results." />

## History

History is **off by default**. Enable it before a run to keep both raw and cleaned
text for comparison. It holds at most **20 entries and 2 MiB in memory**, and
clears when Freehand exits. Copy text you want to keep before quitting.

<LinkCard title="Browse session history" href="../history/" description="Find, compare, copy, or remove retained transcripts." />

## Current result and task settings

**Current result** holds the latest transcript even with history off. **Copy**
recovers a result after failed insertion; **Clear** removes it; **Listen** plays
it when speech is enabled. Starting another recording replaces the Voice result.
Clear or replace a selected file to remove its file result.

Select a row in the task sidebar, or its **Settings** cog, to open detailed
options beside the result. Quick controls save immediately; detailed edits use
**Save**. Changing settings does not alter work already running.

## Finding and adjusting settings

Use **Settings** for **General**, **Shortcuts**, **Overlay**, and **Vocabulary**.
Use each task's **Settings** cog for transcription, audio, cleanup, speech, or
delivery options. **Find settings** searches by page name or terms such as
“glow” and “hotkey”; Enter opens the first match and Escape clears the filter.

Leaving unsaved options offers **Save**, **Discard**, or **Keep editing**. A
failed save keeps the draft and previous applied settings. The
[workspace guide](../workspace/#save-settings) explains quick controls, detailed
options, and model pickers.

## Action feedback

Open **Details** beside an error to see the explanation and relevant settings.
After correcting the problem:

| Failed action | Recovery |
| --- | --- |
| Voice recording | **Record again**; discarded microphone audio cannot be retried |
| Audio file | **Retry** the selected file |
| Speech composer | **Try again** with the text currently in the editor; the draft is preserved |

See [Troubleshooting](../troubleshooting/) for the visible failure category.

## Status overlay and tray

The passive overlay shows recording, processing, and copy-required state without
taking focus. Error and **needs copy** notices disappear after five seconds;
the result or error remains available in Freehand. Configure it under
**Settings → Overlay**, or add [live captions](../live-transcription/).

Click the Windows tray or macOS menu-bar icon for recording controls, the Voice
connection and model, and the latest result with **Copy**. Choose **Open Freehand**
for the full workspace. Escape or clicking outside dismisses the panel without
cancelling work. On Windows, focus the destination and use your shortcut when
you want to choose exactly where recording will deliver.

:::note[Closing the window keeps Freehand running]
Resolve any unsaved settings, then close to hide the workspace. Right-click the
tray or menu-bar icon and choose **Quit** to exit and cancel active work. Let an
audio export finish first: interrupting it can leave a partial WAV file.
:::

## Appearance

Choose **System**, **Light**, or **Dark** under **Settings → General**. Windows
also offers Mica. Native appearance changes take effect after quitting from the
tray and reopening Freehand.
