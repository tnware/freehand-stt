Freehand bundles provider icons locally in the app and website. Brand marks
identify providers; they do not imply endorsement or certify model support.
Generic uses a neutral protocol symbol. Speaches, whisper.cpp, Kokoro-FastAPI,
and openedai-speech currently use Freehand's neutral symbols rather than an
official logo. vLLM-Omni shares the vLLM family mark.
